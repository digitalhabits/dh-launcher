# DH Launcher — privacy policy

*Draft. Last updated: [date of publication].*

DH Launcher collects no personal data.

- **No data leaves your device.** The app makes no network requests. It has no
  analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
- **What the app stores.** The names of the apps you add, how to open them,
  and your settings. These are kept only on your iPhone, in storage that the
  app shares with its own widget. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **No tracking.** The app does not track you across apps or websites.
- **Other apps.** When you tap a name, iOS opens that app (or runs your
  Shortcut). What that app does with data is covered by its own privacy policy.
